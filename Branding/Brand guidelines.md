# VibeWare brand guidelines

The term VibeWare was coined on 5 October 2026. Umberto approved the blue-and-cream floppy logo on the same date. The approved reference is the exact PNG attached to the conversation, stored here as [vibeware-logo.png](vibeware-logo.png), without cropping, recoloring or regeneration.

![Approved VibeWare logo](vibeware-logo.png)

## Visual identity

Use the spelling **VibeWare**, with capital V and W. The pixel-art floppy disk and VW monogram are the primary compact symbol. The VibeWare wordmark is a separate secondary signature, used where the name must stand alone. Its color direction is denim blue, warm cream and dark graphite; these are descriptive colors, not a formally specified print palette.

The requested style is 1980s cyber computing, lo-fi and pixel art, explicitly without neon art. Do not treat cyber as a request for fluorescent colors, glowing outlines or synthwave effects.

## Asset handling

The approved PNG is 1254 by 1254 pixels and contains an alpha channel; its corner is transparent, not a baked black background. Preserve the original file and its transparent canvas. Do not stretch it, add effects or claim it is a vector master.

Proposal 02 in the [archive](../Archive/Logo%20proposals/2026-10-05/Logo%20proposals.md) is the original generated floppy concept. The attachment and the generated file have separate checksums, so both are preserved and identified in the [manifest](../Archive/Logo%20proposals/2026-10-05/manifest.json).

## Archive status

All eight original concepts and their prompts are retained unchanged. Only the floppy direction is approved. Archiving the remaining concepts does not endorse them as compliant with every part of the brief.
