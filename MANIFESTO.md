# VibeWare manifesto

VibeWare is a name for building with intent. It begins with a person deciding what deserves to exist, why it should exist and what “good enough” means. AI can accelerate the route from an idea to working material, but it does not replace the decision to make it useful, legible and honest.

> VibeWare = Human intent, AI, and plenty of tokens ;-)

The word “Vibe” is the direction: taste, context, curiosity and the willingness to try an imperfect first version. “Ware” is the commitment to make something concrete: code, interfaces, documentation, hardware or a tool that another person can actually use.

VibeWare does not mean that everything generated is correct, finished or reviewed. It means the workflow should leave room for judgment: understand the constraints, preserve evidence, test proportionately and make uncertainty visible.
